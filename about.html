ABOUT THE SILENT STORM

The Silent Storm is a digital archive dedicated to exploring the depth, diversity and richness of the African continent. Throught the voices of learned scholars, renowed writers, researchers, storytellers, and cultural enthusiasts, we journey into Africa's stories, history, myths, cultures, traditions, lifestyles, people, places, and happenings.

We seek not only to educate, but to entertain, provoke thought, and awaken a sense of oneness and belonging.

For generations, much of what is African has been dismissed as primitive, while Western ideas and ways of life have often been presented as the standard of literacy, sophistication, and enlightenment. The Silent storm challenges that gaze.

We look inward.

Africa is not merely a point on the map, It is home to diverse tribes, people, and culture, A home that carries knowledge, memory, imagination, resilence, and potential of people from different backgrounds, sharing a collective experience.

We explore Africa on its own terms - not to romanticise it, nor to deny its complexities, but to understand it. To uncover the knowledge buried beneath generations of neglect. To revisit the stories that were forgotten, the traditions that were dismissed, the stories that were overlooked, and the identities that were gradually taught to feel foreign.

That is why we are called The Silent Storm.

A Storm is coming - not necessarilly with noise, but with awakening.

Until every quake and tremor reminds you where your roots began, and its fruits and branches births a future that starts from today.
